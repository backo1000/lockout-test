Privacy and terms, in plain words. Last changed 17 November 2026.

Who runs it

Lockout is made and run by [YOUR NAME], in the United Kingdom. Questions, or to have your data deleted: [CONTACT EMAIL].

What we keep

  • Your email address and password. Used to log you in and to send you a confirm or a password reset email. We never see your password: it is stored scrambled (hashed) by our login service.
  • Your progress. Your level, cosmetics, decks and settings, so you can carry on from another phone or computer.
  • Your player name and rank on the Top 500. These are public: anyone playing can see them.
  • Online matches. While you play friends online, the moves of that match pass through our server. Each player's view of the cards is sealed so only they can read it. Chat messages in a room pass through the same way. Nothing is kept after the room closes.
  • Your device. The game saves some things in your browser (settings, Remember me). Clearing your browser's data removes them.

We don't sell your data, show adverts, or track you round the web.

Who else handles it

Logins, saves and online rooms are run by Supabase. Emails are sent by our email service. The game itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any website, sees your IP address when you load it. The game also loads its font from Google Fonts.

How long, and deleting it

We keep your account until you delete it. To delete it: log in, then press DELETE ACCOUNT on the first screen. Your login, progress and Top 500 rows go straight away and can't be brought back. You can also email us and we'll do it for you. Under UK law you can also ask us for a copy of what we hold about you.

Children

Lockout is meant for players aged 13 and over. If you are under 13, please don't make an account.

The rules of playing

  • Be fair. Don't cheat, use bots, or break the game on purpose, and don't pick a player name or send chat that is rude, hateful or pretends to be someone else. We can remove names, reset ranks or close accounts that do.
  • Gold, cards and cosmetics in the game are part of the game. They have no money value and can't be sold or swapped for real money.
  • We're always changing the game. Things can be added, taken out or rebalanced, and we may wipe the Top 500 at the end of a season.
  • Lockout is given as it is. We try hard to keep it running and your progress safe, but we can't promise it will never break or lose something.
  • Lockout, its art, music and words belong to its maker.
